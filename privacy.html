Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At GTP Hardware, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal or business information you share with us through our website and service channels.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide directly to us when filling out forms or contacting our team. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and physical or job site location.
  • Trade Account Details: Company name, registration details, sector, and credit references.
  • Inquiry & Project Specs: Bills of Quantities (BOQs), architectural plan details, and product quote requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used strictly to facilitate hardware supply and site installation services, including:

  • Processing quote requests, orders, and site technical assessments.
  • Evaluating trade account applications and setting credit/volume terms.
  • Communicating order status, deliveries, or account updates via email, phone, or WhatsApp.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or commercial data to third parties. We may only share information with certified installation technicians or delivery partners solely for the purpose of completing your order or site service.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational precautions to prevent the loss, misuse, or alteration of your personal details. Access to customer inquiry data is restricted to authorized GTP Hardware staff.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate information, or ask us to delete your details from our customer communication lists.
