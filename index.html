How a job runs

The same four steps whether it is one leaking tap or a full rewire.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Call, WhatsApp a photo, or send your materials list. Most enquiries come back the same working day.

  2. We quote in writing

    Materials and labour priced separately. Site visits are free within Harare.

  3. We book the date

    You get a named technician and a time window, not a whole day of waiting.

  4. You sign it off

    We test the work with you, clear up, and hand over your warranty paperwork.
